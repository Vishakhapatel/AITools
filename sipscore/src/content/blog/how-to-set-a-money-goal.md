"I want to be financially free." "I want to build wealth." Beautiful. Also useless — because you can't plan for a vibe. A goal your money can act on needs three boring things: a **number**, a **deadline**, and a **reality check**. Here's how to build one. 🎯

## Step 1: Name a real number, not a feeling

"Enough to be comfortable" isn't a goal; it's a mood. Convert it:

- Retirement → *"₹5 crore by age 60"*
- House → *"₹25 lakh down payment in 7 years"*
- Kid's college → *"₹40 lakh in 15 years"*

The moment there's a rupee figure and a date, math has something to grab onto. If you don't know the number yet, estimate roughly and refine later — a rough number beats no number.

## Step 2: Adjust for the inflation trap

Here's where most people quietly sabotage themselves: they plan for today's prices. But ₹40 lakh in 15 years won't buy what ₹40 lakh buys now — inflation eats the difference. A goal that ignores inflation is a goal you'll *technically* hit and still fall short on.

So always plan in **future rupees**, not today's. (Our calculator does this automatically — you enter today's value and it inflates the target for you. More on why in [the inflation piece](/blog/inflation-is-the-goal-you-forgot/).)

## Step 3: Work backwards to a monthly number

A goal becomes doable the second it's a monthly SIP instead of a scary lump sum. "₹40 lakh in 15 years" is intimidating; *"₹8,000-ish a month"* is a Tuesday. Working backwards from the goal to the monthly amount is the whole trick — big target, small repeatable habit.

## Step 4: The reality check nobody does

This is the step that separates a *plan* from a *wish*: does the monthly number actually fit your budget? Most calculators skip this and hand you a figure you can't afford, which just breeds guilt.

If the honest answer is "no," you don't abandon the goal — you **adjust a lever**:

- give it more time,
- aim a little lower,
- step up your SIP as your salary grows,
- or find more to invest.

A goal you can afford beats a perfect goal you'll quit in month three.

## The quick checklist

- [ ] A specific rupee **amount**
- [ ] A **deadline**
- [ ] Adjusted for **inflation**
- [ ] Converted to a **monthly SIP**
- [ ] **Affordability-checked** against your budget
